Punk//Body Studio is a body editor for Cyberpunk 2077: shape V&apos;s and NPC bodies with sliders, refit your clothes to
them, and install the result as a mod.

## The Problem

Changing a body in Cyberpunk 2077 normally means installing someone else&apos;s body mod and accepting their proportions
wholesale. Anything beyond that is mesh work in Blender, and the moment the body changes, every garment has to be refit
by hand or it clips straight through. Body mods also tend to overwrite the same game files, so two of them installed at
once quietly fight each other.

## The Solution

Body Studio turns that into sliders. It reads the bodies and clothes from your own install, reshapes them live in a 3D
preview, refits the wardrobe to whatever you shaped, and packs the lot into a single mod archive.

## Key Features

- **Slider-driven bodies**: 30+ sliders in eight groups for female V (all three character-creator breast sizes), male
  V, and 15 NPC body types. Change one NPC body and every NPC wearing it changes with it.
- **Clothes that actually fit**: V&apos;s garments are refit to the shaped body and packed into the same mod, covering the
  base game, Phantom Liberty, and any clothing mods already installed.
- **Physics**: optional breast, glute and cloth physics fitted to the shaped body, with per-axis intensity, previewed
  on the game&apos;s own walk cycle.
- **One-click deploy**: builds and installs to `archive\pc\mod` or as a REDmod, after checking for body mods that would
  collide and offering to move them aside.
- **Export**: bodies out as game meshes or glTF (`.glb`) for Blender, slider sets as shareable presets, and `.punk`
  projects that keep every body and setting for later.
- **Works with existing body mods**: imports bodies from archives such as VTK Vanilla HD, Hyst Angel and Hyst EBB, then
  treats them like game bodies for sliders, refit and physics.
- **Non-destructive by design**: the game&apos;s own files are never edited; everything lands in one removable archive.

## Built With

A Windows desktop app with a real-time 3D viewport, driving the WolvenKit command-line tool for game archive I/O. The
installer pulls WolvenKit and a private .NET runtime from their official sources into its own folder, so nothing is
installed system-wide and no administrator rights are needed. Seat of the whole thing is glTF: bodies round-trip through
it, which is what makes Blender export fall out for free.

Freeware rather than open source, distributed from the project site and Nexus Mods, with opt-in anonymous usage
statistics that never include files, paths or slider values.
